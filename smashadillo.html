How it plays

Tap
Launch off the ramp, then hop over stones.
Tap in the air
Spin-smash straight down through the roof.
Tap in the ring
A PERFECT SPIN hits harder and pays more.
Hit the crack
Land on the glowing crack and the building falls into the next one. That's a CHAIN, and chains pay the most.

Between runs

Spend your coins on a bigger ramp, tougher armour (leather, bark, stone, iron, steel, gold) and more income.

Pick a new look: pangolin, hedgehog, snowball, boulder or bowling ball.
