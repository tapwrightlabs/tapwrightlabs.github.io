Privacy

Last updated: 19 September 2026

Our web games and this website don't ask for, collect or send any personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no chat, no analytics and no cookies.

Who we are

Tapwright Labs is an independent game studio. This policy covers this website and the web versions of our games. You can reach us at tapwrightlabs@gmail.com.

Our web games

  • The game saves a few things in your browser so it remembers them next time: your coins, upgrades, skins (and skin tries), missions and best runs, plus a few settings: sound on or off, your control scheme, and whether you chose to play with the phone upright.
  • That save stays on your device. We never receive it. Clearing your browser's site data deletes it.
  • There are no accounts and nothing is sent to a server of ours. We don't run one.
  • Reward videos in our games are optional: you choose to watch one to get something in the game, and declining never locks anything.

When you play one of our games on a game website run by someone else, that website shows the game and any videos, and its own privacy policy covers what it collects. We'll name each one here when a game is released there.

This website

  • No analytics, no cookies, no ads, no trackers, no third-party scripts and no web fonts. Every file comes from this site.
  • The gameplay video only downloads if you press play.
  • Some pages link to our videos on YouTube. Nothing from YouTube loads on this site; if you follow such a link, YouTube's own privacy policy applies there.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical data such as your IP address to keep the service running and secure. That is covered by GitHub's own privacy statement, and we don't receive those logs.

If you e-mail us

We receive your e-mail address and what you write, and use them only to reply. We delete the conversation when it's no longer needed, or sooner if you ask.

Children

Our games collect no personal information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

We hold no personal data about you from the games or this site, so there's nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you've e-mailed us, you can ask us to delete that conversation. If you live in the EU or UK, you can also complain to your data protection authority.

Mobile apps (note dated 19 September 2026)

We haven't released any mobile apps yet. When we do, they will show optional reward videos through Google AdMob, which collects some device data to show and measure those ads, and asks for your permission first where the law requires it. We will update this policy, with a new date, before any app is released.

Changes

If anything here changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top before the change takes effect.
