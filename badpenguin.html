Bad Penguin

Bowl the whole flock into the sea.

Coming soon

You're the bad penguin in the bandana. Hold to charge a belly-slide, let go, and bowl through the huddle like pins. Push the whole flock off the floe before your fish run out.

Gameplay video

Captured from the current build, without sound. A x11 push, the leopard seal crossing the floe, the walrus charging, and The Big Berg. The video (12 MB) only downloads when you press play.

How it plays

Hold
Crouch and charge. Holding also brakes you before the edge.
Let go
Belly-slide the way you aim. Everyone you hit bowls into the next one.
Fish
Every push costs a fish. Clear the floe before they run out.
Chicks
Leave the little yellow ones alone. And watch out for the walrus.

Two ways to be bad

Levels: ten floes, from The Pond to The Big Berg, with heavy emperor penguins, rocks to bank off and a leopard seal that crosses the ice.

Arena Solo: you against seven other bad penguins. Three 60-second rounds, and a point for everyone you push into the sea.

Plays in your browser on desktop and phone. We'll post where to play here as soon as it's out.
